Rusty's Pigtail – Serial Terminal

Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

This policy covers Rusty's Pigtail – Serial Terminal, developed by Christoffer Zakrisson (rustypig91), including the Microsoft Store and direct-download editions.

Pigtail processes serial data on your computer. The app does not upload your serial captures, commands, settings, or diagnostic logs to the developer, and does not include advertising, usage analytics, or automatic crash-report uploads. No Pigtail account is required.

Data the app uses

  • Serial communication: Pigtail reads data from the serial devices you connect and sends commands or file contents you choose to transmit. Received bytes are automatically saved in local session captures, independently of search filters and display mode. Device output may contain personal or sensitive information.
  • Device information: Serial port paths, USB vendor and product IDs, device serial numbers, manufacturer and product names, connection names, timestamps, and port settings are used to identify devices, reconnect them, and restore sessions. This information can be saved with settings and captures.
  • Preferences and saved work: The app stores settings, connections, workspace layouts, macros and their command text, highlight rules, and plot definitions locally to restore your work.
  • Files and clipboard: The app reads files you select for transmission and writes exports to destinations you choose. Copy and paste actions use your system clipboard.
  • Diagnostics: Local application and crash logs can include the app version, graphics hardware and driver information, file paths, error details, and crash backtraces. These help investigate failures and are not automatically sent to the developer.

Storage, security, and deletion

Settings, captures, and diagnostic logs are stored in your user profile. The Microsoft Store edition uses separate application folders from the direct-download edition. Use Settings → Folders to find the locations used by your installation.

Pigtail does not add encryption to these local files or to serial communication. File access is governed by your operating system and filesystem permissions. Backups, synced folders, and the connected device may retain their own copies outside Pigtail's control.

Saved captures have no fixed expiry date. The configured log-history limit controls cleanup of older, inactive captures; active captures remain complete. Clear console discards the selected connection's history and requests capture cleanup. It does not delete exports, settings, or diagnostic logs, and is not secure erasure.

To remove all locally stored Pigtail data, find its folders, close the app, and delete its configuration and data files. Delete exports and backups separately. Uninstallation may leave files behind. The developer cannot access or delete files held only on your computer.

Network access and third-party services

Microsoft Store edition: The app does not use the GitHub updater. Microsoft Store and Windows manage distribution and updates and may process store, installation, and diagnostic information under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. These services operate independently of Pigtail's local data handling.

Direct-download editions: Startup update checks contact GitHub by default and can be disabled in Settings. Manual update checks also contact GitHub; choosing Update downloads release files from GitHub and its download infrastructure. Requests expose your IP address and request details, including the app version in its user-agent, to those services. They do not include serial captures, device identities, command text, or local logs. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Opening project, support, donation, or Store links uses your browser or the corresponding system app. Information you provide to those services is handled under their policies. Commands and file transfers are sent to the serial device you select; any further processing by that device is outside Pigtail's control.

Information you choose to share

If you email the developer or submit an issue, the developer receives the contact information, message, and attachments you provide and uses them to respond and investigate the report. Support correspondence may be retained while needed to resolve and follow up on the request. You can request deletion of correspondence held by the developer using the contact address below, subject to applicable legal obligations.

GitHub issues are public. Remove personal data, passwords, tokens, and sensitive device output before sharing logs or captures. The app does not sell personal information or share local app data with advertisers.
